Need help with one of our apps? Write to **support@laminar.design** with the app name, your device, and what happened. We reply within a few working days.

## Common questions

**Where are my answers stored?**
On your iPhone. If you turn on iCloud for the app, they also sync through your own iCloud account. We never see them.

**How do I delete my data?**
Delete the app. If you use iCloud sync, also remove the app's data in Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Storage.

**How do I move my data to a new phone?**
Turn on iCloud sync for the app before you switch, or restore from an iPhone backup.

**Are the results a diagnosis?**
No. The app is for self-reflection. Its questions come from open research tools, and it asks them a few at a time over many days, which differs from how they were originally tested. Treat results as a starting point for thinking, not as a clinical measure.

**I'm going through a hard time.**
This app isn't a substitute for care from a qualified professional. If you are in distress, please reach out to someone you trust or a local helpline.

**Can I suggest a feature?**
Yes, please. Write to support@laminar.design.
