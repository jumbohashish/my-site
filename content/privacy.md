This policy covers laminar.design and apps published by Laminar Design Private Limited ("we", "us"). In short: we collect as little as we can, and our apps keep your data on your device.

## Our apps

**What we collect:** nothing. Your answers, results and any notes you write are stored on your device.

**iCloud:** if you turn on iCloud sync, your data is stored in your own iCloud account, under Apple's privacy terms. We cannot access it.

**No accounts, no tracking.** Our apps have no sign-up, no advertising, no analytics, and no third-party tracking tools.

**Deleting your data:** delete the app. If you use iCloud sync, also remove the app's data from your iCloud storage. Because we hold nothing, there is nothing for us to delete.

**Children:** our apps are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

## This website

We do not use cookies, analytics or advertising on this site.

The site is hosted by Cloudflare, which may briefly process technical information such as your IP address to deliver pages and protect against abuse. Its typefaces are served by Google Fonts, which receives the same kind of technical information when a page loads. See the privacy policies of [Cloudflare](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/) and [Google](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## If you email us

We use your email only to reply to you, and we don't share it. We delete old correspondence we no longer need.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above. If a future version of an app needs to collect data, we'll say so here and in the app before it does.
