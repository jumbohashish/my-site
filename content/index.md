LaminarDesign is committed to supporting the individual in their developmental journey across the lifespan.

We make quiet software for that journey: tools that help people understand themselves and decide well, then get out of the way. Laminar flow is smooth, layered movement without turbulence. That's how we want your daily life to feel like.

## What we are making

**CentreAligned** – a daily self-reflection app for iPhone. A few questions a day, drawn from open, well-studied personality research. Over weeks, your answers add up to a clear, readable picture of how you tend to think and act. Your answers stay on your device.

**LittleDecider®** is a real-time decision-support tool for your frequent everyday decisions. It helps you in the moment of choosing, when a clear head matters most.

## What we believe

**Less is more.** The smallest amount of technology that solves the problem is usually the right amount.

**Your data is yours.** We collect only what a feature needs, and where we can, nothing at all.

**Honest over impressive.** Our tools offer reflection, not diagnosis. We say so clearly.

**Calm by design.** No streak guilt, no endless feeds, no notifications for their own sake.
